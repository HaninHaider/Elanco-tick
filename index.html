Prevention Guide

Stay Tick Aware

Four simple steps to help protect yourself and your pets.

  1. 01

    Before You Go

    Dress for Protection

    Wear long sleeves, long trousers and light-coloured clothing to help spot ticks more easily.

  2. 02

    While Outdoors

    Stick to Clear Paths

    Avoid brushing against long grass and dense vegetation where ticks may be present.

  3. 03

    After Your Walk

    Check Yourself & Your Pets

    Check for ticks after outdoor activities, including behind the knees, around the hairline and on pets.

  4. 04

    If You Find a Tick

    Remove It Safely

    Use fine-tipped tweezers or a tick-removal tool, then clean the bite area.

When to Seek Medical Advice

If you've been bitten by a tick and develop symptoms such as a spreading rash, fever or flu-like illness, seek advice from a healthcare professional.
